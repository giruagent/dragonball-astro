01 / The beginnings

Great adventures.
Unexpected beginnings.

Before the Dragon Balls

Toriyama was already known for the hit manga Dr. Slump before he created Dragon Ball. His path to that new adventure included Dragon Boy, a one-shot made while Dr. Slump was still being serialized. [2]

The official Toriyama Showcase describes Dragon Boy as a spiritual predecessor to Dragon Ball and recounts its kung-fu-movie inspiration. A short experiment became part of a much bigger creative journey. [3]

02 / A new adventure begins

1984.

Weekly Shonen Jump · Issue No. 51

The beginning of
Dragon Ball.

Toriyama’s Dragon Ball began in Weekly Shonen Jump in 1984, issue No. 51. The official retrospective traces young Goku’s early adventures back to those first pages. [2]

This is the connection at the heart of this tribute: behind the characters and adventures celebrated throughout this site is Toriyama’s work as their manga creator.

In remembrance / 1955 — 2024

The adventure lives on.

Shueisha’s announcement records Toriyama’s passing on March 1, 2024, at age 68. Its tributes remember both the artist and the person behind the work. This page celebrates that creative legacy. [1]
